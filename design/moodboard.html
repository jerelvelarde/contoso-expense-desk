What we leave out

The common causes of "ugly" and "AI-generated" in agent demos.

A giant number nobody readsStatus, not the amount, is the most important thing in the header. The amount is 26px sans, tabular.
A permanent chat columnThe employee talks in Teams. The dashboard shows the agent's work, not a second chat.
Fields before they existDetails appear once the agent has read and checked them, and lock after the decision.
Raw JSON by defaultEach tool call is a sentence. Its args and result open from the tool chip.
Editorial stylingNo cream paper, serif headlines or tracked mono eyebrows. One serif sentence for the learned rule.
Spinners and mode labelsLive steps pulse. The top bar says what the agent is doing (Working, Paused), never how it is hosted.
