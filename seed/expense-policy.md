# Contoso Travel & Expense Policy (excerpt)

Version 2026.2 · Owner: Finance Operations

## Hotels

| Rule | Location | Cap per night |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HOTEL-LDN | London | £250 |
| HOTEL-UK | Rest of the UK | £150 |
| HOTEL-INTL | Outside the UK | £200 (GBP equivalent) |

- Rates are per night, excluding taxes and breakfast.
- A request at or under the cap is approved automatically by the expense agent.
- A request over the cap needs the employee's line manager to approve it in Teams.
- Finance can correct request details (rate, nights, dates, cost centre) before the manager decides.

## Learned approvals

The expense agent may apply a learned rule when CopilotKit Automatic Learning has observed a consistent manager decision pattern. A learned rule:

- applies to one category and location,
- never raises a cap by more than 50%,
- is shown to Finance with the approvals it was learned from,
- is cited by the agent every time it is used.
